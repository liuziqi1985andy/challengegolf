Programs

Play all year

Our weekly programs are free for participants. New participants and volunteers are always welcome to visit a session before deciding to join.

May – early October

Summer Program

Weekly sessions on the driving range and golf course, tailored for people with physical limitations. Volunteers and our golf cart help participants get from the parking lot to our reserved tees.

WhenMondays, 1:00–2:30 pm (except statutory holidays)
SeasonEarly May to the start of October
WhereTee Zone Driving Range, 4105 Bronte Rd (north of Hwy 407), Oakville
CostFree

Twice a season

Golf Tournaments

Two tournaments a year at Granite Ridge Golf Club, a chance to get out on the course and enjoy a full day of golf. Each participant is paired with a volunteer.

WhereGranite Ridge Golf Club
How oftenTwo tournaments each season
FormatGroups of four, best ball, double-bogey maximum. Each participant is paired with a volunteer.
CostSubsidized fee

Come and visit us first

We encourage all new participants and volunteers to come out and visit a session before deciding to join. Spaces are limited, and registration forms are sent out each season, so contact us to arrange a visit.

Contact us
What to expect

Your first session

  • No golf experience is needed. Volunteers offer advice, tuition and encouragement.
  • Clubs and balls are provided, and tees are kept full by volunteers.
  • Our golf cart can take you from the parking lot to the reserved tees.
  • Participants arrange their own transportation to the site.
  • Participants should be able to manage their basic needs independently or with a little help.
