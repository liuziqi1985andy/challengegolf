About us

Our story

Golf for the Physically Challenged, also known as Challenge Golf, is a registered Canadian charity based in Oakville, Ontario.

Our mission

Empowering active, fulfilling lives

Our mission is to empower community members facing physical limitations, aiding them in their journey towards a more active and fulfilling life, both physically and mentally.

We strive to expand opportunities for fellowship by providing a dedicated space, necessary equipment, and guidance.

Our team of volunteers is committed to offering advice, tuition, and encouragement, enabling participants to learn and play the game of golf to the fullest extent of their abilities.

History

Our journey

From a driving range in 1990 to a year-round program today. We're grateful to everyone who has contributed along the way. Together, let's keep making our community an even better place.

1990

Where it all began. Challenge Golf starts out at the Family Golf Driving Range in Oakville.

1992

A registered charity. Golf for the Physically Challenged becomes an officially registered Canadian charity.

2013

A new home at Tee Zone. We move to Tee Zone Driving Range, owned by BOT Construction, which has generously let us use its range free of charge ever since.

2023

Tournament days. We launch two annual tournaments at Granite Ridge Golf Club.

2024

Golf all year round. Our winter indoor program begins at Birdies Indoor Golf in Oakville, making Challenge Golf a year-round program.

2024

A holiday tradition. We hold our first Christmas gathering, bringing members together for the holiday season.

Today

Still growing. Weekly summer and winter programs, tournaments, social events and new caregiver support, all run by volunteers. In 2027 we celebrate 35 years. Join the celebration →

Who we serve

Our participants

Our participants are valued members of the local community living with neurological, physical and developmental limitations. We cater to their varied and specific needs and provide a supportive environment for their golfing journey.

No previous golf experience or fees are required. Many participants come from Oakville, and we warmly welcome people from across the Greater Toronto Area.

Visitors curious about the program are always welcome to drop by a session.
