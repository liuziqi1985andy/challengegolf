Read the 2027 welcome letter

Hello everyone,

We hope you all enjoyed our summer session! To help everyone stay active during the winter months, our Winter Indoor Golf Program will begin again on Monday, January 11, 2027. The program runs Mondays from 1:00 to 3:00 pm at Birdies Indoor Golf (504 Iroquois Shore Rd, Unit 8, Oakville), for 13 sessions between January 11 and April 19, 2027 (no golf on Family Day, February 15, or Easter Monday, March 29).

Celebrating 35 years! Join us at our 35th Anniversary Celebration on Saturday, April 17, 2027, 2–9 pm at the Oakville Conference Centre. Participants, volunteers, family and friends are all welcome. Get your tickets →
