The celebration

Thirty-five years of believing and achieving

Since 1992, Challenge Golf has helped people living with physical challenges enjoy the game. On April 17, 2027 we're celebrating with the participants, families, volunteers and supporters who made it happen.

Join us for an afternoon and evening of stories, celebration and community, and help us raise funds for the next 35 years.

Highlights

  • An exhibition of Challenge Golf's history, with stories from our participants and work by local artists
  • The first-ever Richard Malinski Award
  • Speeches from board members, participants and local leaders

Special thanks to the Oakville Community Foundation for their support with ticketing.
