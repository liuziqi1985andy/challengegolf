Volunteer

Be part of the team

Volunteers create a friendly, safe environment. Chatting with participants and helping with equipment is at the heart of it.

  • Set up the Challenge Golf section and semi-automated tees at Tee Zone
  • Keep tees stocked with golf balls and hand out clubs
  • Bring water to participants
  • Drive participants from the parking lot to our tees by golf cart
